First measure,final stitch.

Four steps, usually two visits. Bridal pieces get a third fitting.

  2. 01

    Consultation

    We talk about the occasion, how you like things to fall, fabrics and the date you need it by.

    ≈ 30 min · in studio or video
  3. 02

    Measurements

    Twenty-two points taken by hand, then drafted into a paper pattern that's yours to keep.

    ≈ 20 min · pattern within 2 days
  4. 03

    Cut, stitch & fit

    A basted trial fitting first, so the silhouette is right before the final seams go in.

    1–2 fittings · 7–14 days
  5. 04

    Final finish

    Hand-felled hems, pressed with steam and checked on you once more before it goes home.

    Pick-up or delivery in Delhi NCR
